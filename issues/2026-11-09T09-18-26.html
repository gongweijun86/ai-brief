07｜S4000完成4000米级空中风电试飞

来源（X） · @PDChina · 2026-11-09 16:30

中文译文

世界上首套4,000米级机载风力发电系统于周五在中国中部湖南省岳阳市完成了试飞，标志着此类系统首次在城市环境中完成测试。

该由中国研发的 S4000 系统利用氦气平台将轻型发电设备升至数千米高空，在那里利用高空风资源发电，并通过系留电缆将电力传输至地面。

S4000 长67米、宽40米、高23米，可输出10至35千伏的电压，为电力基础设施有限的地区提供了一种潜在的清洁能源来源。

原文

The world's first 4,000-meter-class airborne wind power system completed a test flight in Yueyang, central China's Hunan Province, on Friday, marking the first time a system of its kind has been tested in an urban setting.

The China-developed S4000 system uses a helium-filled platform to lift lightweight power-generating equipment thousands of meters into the sky, where it harnesses high-altitude wind resources to generate electricity and transmits it to the ground through a tether cable.

Measuring 67 meters long, 40 meters wide and 23 meters high, the S4000 can deliver output voltages ranging from 10 to 35 kilovolts, offering a potential source of clean power for regions with limited electricity infrastructure.
