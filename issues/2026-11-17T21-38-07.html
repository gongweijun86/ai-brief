04｜GPT-6.2 Astra 破解百年密信

来源（X） · @deredleritt3r · 2026-11-18 02:24

中文译文

我使用 GPT-6.2 Astra破译了 Raymundo Machaín 于 1878 年写给 Juan Silvano Godoi 的信——据我所知，这封信此前从未被破译过。重要的是，GPT-6.2 Astra 在没有任何密钥的情况下破译了这封信。

Godoi 是巴拉圭的一位政治人物，此前因参与刺杀巴拉圭前总统 J.B. Gill 的阴谋而逃离该国。后来在 1878 年，Godoi 购买了一艘船和 700 支枪，沿巴拉圭河溯流而上，试图推翻该国新任总统 Candido Bareiro。这封信是由其同谋 Raymundo Machaín 写给 Godoi 的，内容涉及此次远征的枪支运输。

这封信中只有少数敏感短语是用密码写成的（其余部分是用西班牙语书写的）。多亏了 GPT-6.2 Astra，我们现在知道：

1. 在蒙得维的亚与 Godoi 合作装备这艘船的人（信中也被称为"那个英国人"）是"Brown"——很可能是 Eduardo Brown，根据其他通信记录，他曾与 Godoi 合作装备此次远征。

2. 到达蒙得维的亚并向 Machaín 提供资金的人是"Bravo"——很可能是 Francisco Javier Bravo。密码揭示，Bravo 向 Machaín 展示了"完整而准确的清单"（推测是所购买的武器清单），并向 Machaín 提供资金以"派船[出发]并支付船员薪水"。

3. Machaín 说，一家名为"Folle"的航运公司拒绝在没有收到"正式指示"的情况下处理枪支运输，而他需要 Godoi 出具这些指示。

4. Brown 显然不信任 Bravo："Brown 害怕 Bravo 会给我们玩阴的"，密码这样写道。还有："Brown 希望一切都登记在他名下，以便如果有人试图夺走这些东西，他有权收回。"

Astra 是如何破译这个密码的：

- 首先，它根据信中周围的上下文猜测了三个词："Machaín 通过在明文西班牙语和密码之间切换的方式给了我们线索。他写了 'me mostró una'（'他给我看了一个……'），然后是五个加密符号。Lista（'清单'）是一个猜测。在另一处，'sin antes recibir'（'在先收到……之前'）后面跟着十三个符号，而 Machaín 在同一页后面的明文西班牙语中使用了 instrucciones（'指示'）这个词。第三个猜测是 factura（'发票'）。"

- 然后，模型推导出了密钥：将书写的字符依次按西班牙语字母表向上或向下移动 −7、−11、+13、+8、+4，完美匹配了上述所有单词。

- 当模型意识到密码在每个单词处都会重新开始密钥序列，即使几个词被加密在一起没有空格分隔时，它迎来了"突破"。这使得 GPT-6.2 Astra 完全破译了这封信。

原文

I used GPT-6.2 Astra to decipher Raymundo Machaín's letter to Juan Silvano Godoi, written in 1878 - which, to my knowledge, has never been deciphered before. Significantly, GPT-6.2 Astra deciphered this letter without having access to any key.

Godoi, a Paraguayan politician, had previously fled the country after being involved in a conspiracy to assassinate Paraguay's previous president, J.B. Gill. Later in 1878, Godoi would buy a ship and 700 guns and sail up the Paraguay river in a bid to overthrow the country's new president, Candido Bareiro. This letter, which was written to Godoi by his accomplice, Raymundo Machaín, relates to the shipment of guns for this expedition.

Only a few sensitive phrases in this letter were written in a cipher (the rest is just written in Spanish). Thanks to GPT-6.2 Astra, we now know that:

1. The man working with Godoi in Montevideo to equip the ship (also referred to in the letter as "the Englishman") is "Brown" - likely, Eduardo Brown, who, according to other correspondence, had been working with Godoi on equipping the expedition.

2. The man who arrived in Montevideo and offered Machaín money was "Bravo" - likely Francisco Javier Bravo. The cipher reveals that Bravo showed Machaín the "complete and accurate list" (presumably of the weapons being purchased) and offered Machaín money to "dispatch the [ship] and pay the crew".

3. Machaín says that "Folle", a shipping firm, has refused to handle the shipment of guns without being presented "formal instructions", which he needs from Godoi.

4. Brown apparently doesn't trust Bravo: "Brown is afraid Bravo will play us a dirty trick", the cipher reads. And: "Brown wants everything put in his name so that he has the right to reclaim it if they try to take it away."

How Astra solved this cipher:

- First, it just guessed at three words in the letter based on the surrounding context: "Machaín gave us clues in the way he switched between plain Spanish and cipher. He wrote “me mostró una” (“he showed me a…”) and then five encrypted signs. Lista (“list”) was a guess. Elsewhere, “sin antes recibir” (“without first receiving…”) was followed by thirteen signs, and Machaín used the word instrucciones in clear Spanish later on the same page. A third guess was factura (“invoice”)."

- Then, the model figured out the key: moving the written characters, in order, and up and down the Spanish alphabet by −7, −11, +13, +8, +4, fit all of the above words perfectly.

- The model had a "breakthrough" when it realized that the cipher had restarted the key at every word, even when a few words were encrypted together without spaces. This let GPT-6.2 Astra completely decipher the letter.

09｜Valve AMDGPU补丁减游戏卡顿

来源（X） · @sysxplore · 2026-11-17 22:21

中文译文

Valve 正在为 Linux 游戏中最恼人的问题之一——卡顿（stutter）——开发一个内核修复。

Valve Linux 图形驱动团队的 Natalie Vock 今天发布了 v2 版的 10 个补丁 AMDGPU 系列，重新设计了驱动程序处理 GPU 虚拟内存更新的方式。

背景：许多较新的 Windows 游戏严重依赖"稀疏绑定"（sparse bindings），即动态映射和取消映射 GPU 内存块。目前 AMDGPU 驱动程序会将这些更新与其他所有 GPU 工作同步处理，且每次只能处理一个调用。用她的话说，繁重的稀疏绑定负载"可能让 gfx/compute 提交停顿相当长的时间，导致非常明显的卡顿和顿挫。"

她的修复增加了一个新的 AMDGPU_GEM_VA2 接口，可以将多个映射更改批量为一次调用，并使用显式同步。补丁说明表示，频繁进行稀疏操作的应用程序，"特别是某些在 Proton 下运行的游戏"，应该能看到"卡顿的显著减少"。

目前还没有基准测试数据。该系列约 1,100 行代码，正在 amd-gfx 邮件列表上进行审查。v1 版本在五月份就已发布，因此目前尚不清楚这是否会进入 Linux 7.5。

原文

Valve is working on a kernel fix for one of the most annoying problems in Linux gaming: stutter.

Natalie Vock of Valve's Linux graphics driver team posted v2 of a 10-patch AMDGPU series today that reworks how the driver handles GPU virtual memory updates.

The background: many newer Windows games lean heavily on "sparse bindings", mapping and unmapping chunks of GPU memory on the fly. Right now the AMDGPU driver syncs those updates against every other piece of GPU work and handles them one call at a time. In her words, heavy sparse binding load "can stall the gfx/compute submissions for quite a long while, leading to very noticeable stutters and hitches."

Her fix adds a new AMDGPU_GEM_VA2 interface that batches many mapping changes into one call and uses explicit sync. The patch notes say apps that hammer sparse operations, "particularly some games running under Proton," should see "significant reductions in stuttering."

There are no benchmark numbers yet. The series is about 1,100 lines of code and is up for review on the amd-gfx mailing list. v1 was posted back in May, so it's still unclear whether this lands in Linux 7.5.
